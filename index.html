Project 1

During the previous semester, Spring 2026, I was a part of a four man team to code and calibrate a CyBot such that it could navigate an obstacle filled test course without the operators seeing it themselves. The CyBot used radar and Infrared sensors to determine an object's size and distance, as well as sense changes in the floor to detect any holes. The CyBot was also equipped with bump sensors for any objects not tall enough to be detected by the infrared or radar.
